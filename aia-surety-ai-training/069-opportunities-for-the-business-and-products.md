# Opportunities for the business and products

- Faster, more consistent support for agents.
- Less rework from incomplete submissions.
- Easier access to relevant procedures and records.
- Better document assistance inside existing software.

**Potential value: service quality, capacity, retention, and differentiation.**

## Speaker notes

AIA's website describes an in-house development team and products including AIMS, Accolade, BailVision, Certificate Exchange, and ePowers. Use that as context for possible future capabilities, not evidence that those products currently contain AI or expose suitable APIs.

Illustrative ideas: source-linked procedure search, a submission-preparation assistant, or a document-comparison view that helps an employee review changes. Validate customer demand and technical feasibility before treating any idea as a product plan.

Separate internal efficiency from customer value. A tool that reduces staff time can also improve response times or free capacity for higher-value work. New revenue or improved retention is a hypothesis to measure, not an automatic result of adding AI.

Ask which convenience customers or agents would actually value, and which decisions should stay with accountable staff.
