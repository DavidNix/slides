# Who is the best at design?

The previous slide said "pick the model for the job" — for design, the job has a leader:

- **Claude is the best at design**, and Anthropic backs it with a dedicated product, **Claude Design**.
- Claude Design turns a chat into **polished visual work**: designs, interactive prototypes, slides, and one-pagers.
- It is aimed at exactly the "no designer on the team" gap — product mockups, pitch decks, and one-pagers produced by chatting.
- Claude Design now works inside any conversation with Claude, so you describe what you want and get visual output you can edit and share.

**For visual work, start with Claude — you may not need a design tool at all.**

## Speaker notes

Land the connection with the last slide: the general answer was "it depends," but design is a task where the gap is real and widely observed — Claude's visual output leads the field, and Anthropic built a whole product around it (launched April 2026 by Anthropic Labs, powered by their most capable vision model).

Name the uses that matter to this room: Marketing can draft one-pagers and social visuals; anyone can mock up a proposal layout, a flyer, or a slide deck by describing it. No code, no design tool — you chat, review, and refine.

Caveats to say out loud: it started as a research preview and is still evolving; treat every output as a first draft to verify, per the verification habits from the Security section — a design can look polished and still contain wrong facts or invented text. And the "best" label can shift with any release; re-check before betting a workflow on it.
