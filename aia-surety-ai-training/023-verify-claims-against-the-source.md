# Verify claims against the source

For each important claim:

1. Find the actual supporting record or passage.
2. Confirm it applies to this case and current version.
3. Check that it supports the claim as written.
4. Preserve missing facts and contradictions.

*Intentionally flawed training draft:*

> "Delivery and payment are verified. The freight claim is approved."

Source facts:

- The receiver confirmed delivery in the fictional exercise.
- The broker reported payment, but the supporting attachment is missing.
- No claim approval is recorded.

**What must change before this draft can be used? A citation must earn your trust by leading to evidence.**

## Speaker notes

Demonstrate the difference between an attached document, a retrieved excerpt, and the model's interpretation. Even a real citation can point to a passage that does not support the claim.

Use the Friday example: the agent requested Friday. That proves a request was made, not that the company committed to it. Use the checklist example: a requirement in DEMO-COMMERCIAL-01 is relevant only because that is the checklist assigned to this fictional submission.

Ask whether the source is authoritative, current, complete, and applicable. Retrieval improves access to evidence; it does not guarantee the right source was retrieved or applied.

Work the flawed draft with the room — it was deliberately written for teaching, not a captured model failure. The receiver's reply supports delivery. The broker's payment statement does not replace the missing payment evidence. Neither establishes approval of the claim. The [presenter answer key](research/demo-packets/freight-identity-review/presenter/answer-key.md) identifies the supporting records.

**Corrected draft:** "The receiver confirmed delivery. The broker reported payment, but the supporting attachment is missing, so payment remains unverified. Claim approval is not established in the available record."

A fluent summary can collapse several different statuses into a single unsupported conclusion — the useful check is to compare each claim with its evidence.

For consequential decisions, have an accountable reviewer resolve missing or conflicting information. An appropriate answer may be "not established by the available record."
