# What LLMs are good at

- **Summarization:** long documents in, short brief out — a 60-page claim file becomes two paragraphs.
- **Data analysis:** finding patterns in tables and text — trends, outliers, groupings.
- **Research:** gathering from many sources and synthesizing one answer.
- **Classification:** sorting items into known categories — inquiry, complaint, submission error.
- **Extraction** unstructured data like documents into structured

**Every strength is pattern matching: recognize the shape of the thing, reproduce it.**
