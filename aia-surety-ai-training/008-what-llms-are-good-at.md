# What LLMs are good at

Four strengths — and all four are the same underlying skill:

- **Summarization:** long documents in, short brief out — a 60-page claim file becomes two paragraphs.
- **Data analysis:** finding patterns in tables and text — trends, outliers, groupings.
- **Research:** gathering from many sources and synthesizing one answer.
- **Classification:** sorting items into known categories — inquiry, complaint, submission error.

**Every strength is pattern matching: recognize the shape of the thing, reproduce it.**

## Speaker notes

This slide lands the payoff of "learned patterns": what pattern matching buys you in daily work.

Each strength is the same move at different scales — recognize a pattern learned from training data and reproduce it. Summarization reproduces the shape of a long document in short form. Analysis recognizes groupings and outliers. Research recognizes relevant material and stitches it together. Classification recognizes which known bucket an item belongs to.

Anchor to the room: the staff survey shows these are already the most common uses — rewording letters, summarizing claim files and carrier contracts, researching regulations, drafting replies. Affirm that these are the *right* uses: they play directly to the strength.
