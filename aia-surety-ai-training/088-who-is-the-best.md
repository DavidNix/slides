# Who is the best?

The honest answer is a non-answer:

- "Best" depends on the task, the price, and the boundary you need.
- Today, **Claude and GPT are the best** general models. Google won't catch up to them.

**Pick the model for the job, not the logo.**

## Speaker notes

As of this training, Claude and GPT lead on general work, coding, and agents. Gemini has not caught up.

"Best" shifts every release. The leader for a narrow task, such as cheap summarization or local use, is often different. The habit of evaluating matters more than today's ranking.

Transition: the model matters less than how you use it.
