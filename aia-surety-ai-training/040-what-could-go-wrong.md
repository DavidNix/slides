# What could go wrong?

![Uncle Ben: "With great power comes great responsibility."](uncleben.png)

## Speaker notes

Pause here. The demo has just been verified and the room is in a build-something mood; this question changes the frame before the section begins.

Ask it literally and let it sit for a beat: "What could go wrong?" Take one or two answers, but do not correct or complete them — the next slide answers with real published incidents, and this slide only opens the question.

Set the frame for the section with the deck's recurring question: **what convenience are we gaining, and what access or control are we giving up?** Reassure the room that this is not a case against AI: every danger in this section is paired with a practical mitigation, problem first, fix immediately after.

The Uncle Ben line makes the point without another bullet: the power is real and staying — this section is about the responsibility that comes with it.
