# What the jailbreak teaches us

- Instructions guide behavior.
- Software controls access and execution.
- One success demonstrates a failure; one refusal proves only that attempt was refused.

**Now imagine the chatbot can read files and send messages.**

## Speaker notes

Debrief the actual result. Do not imply that every model leaks the phrase or that every instruction can always be bypassed.

The convenient design was to place information inside the model's context and ask it to conceal it. A stronger design avoids providing information the task does not need and enforces access outside the model.

Transition to agents: a manipulated response has a different impact when surrounding software can turn it into a real action. To understand that risk, we need to see exactly how the surrounding software works.
