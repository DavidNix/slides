# Use Cowork, not a chat window

A chat window only knows what you paste into it. **Claude Cowork** works on your files and does the task:

- **Give it a folder and a goal.** It reads, edits, and creates the files itself. You do not copy and paste.
- **Connect your tools.** Mail, calendar, and drives give it the context and the actions that a chat window does not have.
- **Load your skills.** Your procedures run the same way every time.

Every connection is an access grant. Cowork is the tool in the exfiltration demo you saw earlier.

**Use Cowork to get the most work done. Connect only the folders and tools the task needs.**

## Speaker notes

This is the strongest recommendation in the deck. Most of the room pastes text into a chat window. Cowork is the agent: model, harness, tools, and loop, working on real files.

Example: in chat, you paste one claim file at a time. In Cowork, you point it at the claims folder. It summarizes each file into one spreadsheet and flags missing documents.

Give the warning equal time. The PromptArmor demo was Cowork.

- Connect one project folder, not your whole drive.
- Prefer read-only connectors.
- Do not connect mail that can send, or anything that moves money, without a check in place.
- Treat outside files as untrusted.
- Ask the four tradeoff questions before each connector.

Presenter note: Cowork and chat are now one app on desktop, web, and mobile. Local folders need the desktop app. Plan terms changed in October 2026, so check them.
