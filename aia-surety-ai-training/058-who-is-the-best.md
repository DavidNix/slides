# Who is the best?

The honest answer is a non-answer:

- "Best" depends on the task, the price, and the boundary you need.
- Today, **Claude and GPT are the best** general models. Google won't catch up to them.

**Pick the model for the job — not the logo.**

## Speaker notes

Say it plainly: as of this training, Claude (Anthropic) and GPT (OpenAI) lead on general capability, coding, and agentic work. Gemini has improved but has not caught them.

Then immediately give the nuance: "best" shifts with every release, and the leader for a specific task — cheap summarization, extraction, local deployment — is often not the overall leader. Benchmarks move quarterly; the evaluation habit matters more than today's ranking.

This section exists because the two leaders are not the only serious options. The next slides widen the choice.
