# Find the errors before acting

*Intentionally flawed training draft:*

> “Delivery and payment are verified. The freight claim is approved.”

Source facts:

- The receiver confirmed delivery in the fictional exercise.
- The broker reported payment, but the supporting attachment is missing.
- No claim approval is recorded.

**What must change before this can be used?**

## Speaker notes

Use this static example to revisit the freight review. The flawed wording was deliberately written for teaching; it is not a captured model failure. Give the room a few seconds to identify what the records support, then explain the corrections.

The receiver's reply supports delivery. The broker's payment statement does not replace the missing payment evidence. Neither establishes approval of the claim. The [presenter answer key](research/demo-packets/freight-identity-review/presenter/answer-key.md) identifies the supporting records.

**Corrected draft:** “The receiver confirmed delivery. The broker reported payment, but the supporting attachment is missing, so payment remains unverified. Claim approval is not established in the available record.”

The useful check is to compare each claim with its evidence. A fluent summary can collapse several different statuses into a single unsupported conclusion.
