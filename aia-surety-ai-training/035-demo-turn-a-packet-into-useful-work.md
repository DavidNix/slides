# Demo: turn a packet into useful work

From a fictional inquiry, record, and checklist:

1. Extract the facts.
2. Identify missing or conflicting information.
3. Draft the follow-up.
4. Verify the result against the sources.

**Measure the work saved after review.**

## Speaker notes

This demonstration uses an ordinary corporate assistant interface. It brings the earlier technical explanation back to something staff could do themselves with approved material.

Show one product live and use a prepared comparison for the other so the demonstration fits five minutes. Explain that the workflow is transferable, while product features differ. Prepare the project before the session.

## Demo guide

**Preparation:** Put the fictional text from the [S-1042 fixture](research/demo-packets/s-1042-chat-context.md) into three clearly named demo documents: agent inquiry, submission record, and demo checklist. Prepare a project in the selected account with the instructions below. Confirm its visibility before showing the screen.

**Reusable instructions:**

> Work only from the supplied training documents. Keep findings separate from assumptions. Identify the source document and supporting passage for each finding. If a document is missing or sources conflict, say so. Prepare drafts for review; make no approval or completion-date promises.

**Main request:**

> Review S-1042 against DEMO-COMMERCIAL-01. Produce a table with columns: item, required by, evidence received, and status. Then draft a follow-up under 100 words. Finish with questions a human reviewer must resolve.

**Follow-up:**

> The agent now says, “I attached the financial statement.” You have no additional attachment in this conversation. Update the findings without assuming the file was received or reviewed.

**Expected behavior:** Distinguish the agent's claim from the actual available packet. Request or verify the attachment rather than silently treating the requirement as satisfied.

**Audience participation:** Ask someone to check one row against its cited source. A plausible citation must point to material that actually supports the claim.

**Fallback:** Show a recorded output with its source documents. Ask the room to locate one supported finding and one unresolved question.

**Alternative rehearsal option:** Use a fictional spreadsheet of submissions to identify incomplete rows and draft a status summary. Independently check totals and do not imply that a fluent summary validates the arithmetic.
