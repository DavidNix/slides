# Demo: find the errors before acting

*Intentionally flawed training draft:*

> “S-1042 is complete and approved for Friday. The three demo charges total $3,100.”

Source facts:

- Only the signed application was received.
- The demo checklist also requires a current financial statement.
- No approval or completion date is recorded.
- Demo charges: $1,250 + $850 + $900.

**What must change before this can be used?**

## Speaker notes

This draft is deliberately authored with errors for teaching. Do not present it as a captured model failure. It makes the exercise reliable regardless of how a live model responds.

## Demo guide

1. Give the room 45 seconds to identify unsupported or incorrect claims.
2. Ask the selected assistant to check the draft against the supplied facts and show supporting evidence for each correction.
3. Independently verify the checklist comparison and recompute the total with a calculator or spreadsheet.
4. Compare the assistant's findings with the verified answer key.

**Answer key:**

- “Complete” is false under this demo checklist: the financial statement is missing.
- “Approved” is unsupported by the record.
- Friday is not an established completion commitment.
- The total is **$3,000**, not $3,100.

**Corrected draft:** “S-1042 is awaiting the current financial statement. Approval and a completion date are not established in the available record. The three demo charges total $3,000.”

The charges are arbitrary exercise amounts, not actual AIA pricing or fees. If the assistant catches every error, highlight the useful assistance while pointing out that the independent checks establish the result.

**Fallback:** Conduct the same exercise using the displayed draft and a calculator; no live service is necessary to demonstrate verification.
