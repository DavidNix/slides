# Fall in love with problems, not solutions 

The fear is common: everyone else is using AI to the fullest, and your team is falling behind.

- The pressure is real, and partly useful — it gets recurring problems on the table.
- But "using AI to the fullest" is not the goal. Completed, verified work is.
- Many of the best wins are simple automation: extract, summarize, draft, check — with a person verifying.
- A boring tool that reliably saves 20 minutes a day beats an ambitious agent that fails quietly.

**Name the task and its business value first; then pick the simplest tool that does it safely.**
