# Always-on agents

The big labs now sell their own always-on assistants:

- **Dots (OpenAI)** — each dot gets its own cloud computer and browser and connects to thousands of apps.
- **Grok Bot (xAI)** and **Claude Duet (Anthropic)** — proactive agents that keep a goal, memory, and app connections between conversations.
- They start work without being asked: briefings, drafts, reminders, follow-ups.

## Speaker notes

Started by OpenClaw in early 2026

Branded as personal assitants.

This makes people think AI will take their job.
