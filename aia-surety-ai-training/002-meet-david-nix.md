## Speaker notes

Suggested introduction:

> My background is in building the software behind large businesses. I've written backend and distributed systems for organizations in retail, healthcare, and government — as a contractor, not an employee. Today, I focus on applying AI to business problems, including running AI privately. I'm here to help you understand what this technology does, where it can help your teams, and what changes when you give it business information and tools.

Use one brief experience from your own work to make the introduction personal. Avoid implying that the listed organizations endorse this session.

Use familiar office workflows throughout the training. Demonstrate products you know, and connect the underlying principles to the audience's environment.

After the introduction, walk the takeaways and ask for a quick show of hands: regular AI users, occasional users, and people who have never tried it. Take two short examples of repetitive work and return to them during the closing exercise.
