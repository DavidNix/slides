# A repeatable staff workflow

**Choose → provide → draft → check → use**

- Choose an approved tool for the information involved.
- Provide the minimum relevant context.
- Request a useful, reviewable output.
- Check facts, recipients, and attachments.
- Use the result with clear ownership.

## Speaker notes

Connect the workflow to staff roles: agent support can draft responses; operations can compare documents; managers can prepare training material. Each task has a different source of truth and consequence of error.

The convenience temptation is to upload the whole folder or connect an entire account. Start with the material needed for the specific job. Broad connections can be useful, but the scope should be a conscious choice.

Ask the room which check they would keep if the system saved ten minutes of preparation. Use that question to introduce the verification section.
