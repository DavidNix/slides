# Open-source leaders: DeepSeek and GLM (z.ai)

Two open-weight providers lead the field:

- **DeepSeek** — strong reasoning models at a fraction of frontier prices; R1 showed reinforcement learning can produce competitive open-weight reasoning.
- **GLM (z.ai)** — capable agentic and coding models from Zhipu, usable via API or self-hosted.
- Open weights mean you can download, inspect, and run the model where your data must stay.

**Open weight means you can run the model yourself — if you can host it.**

## Speaker notes

DeepSeek's R1 demonstrated that an open-weight model trained with reinforcement learning could compete near the frontier, and at a price that disrupted the market. Zhipu's GLM line, served through z.ai, targets agentic and coding work and is a credible open alternative.

"Open weight" means the trained parameters are downloadable. You can run them on your own hardware or at a provider of your choice, audit what you are running, and avoid per-token lock-in. It does not mean the training data or the license are unconstrained — read both.

These two names are the practical answer when someone asks for the best open-source models today. The next slide answers the follow-up: "who runs them for me?"
