# Costs are rising — plan for it

OpenAI's top ChatGPT plan went from **$200 to $500 a month** in September 2026.

**Expect costs to keep rising, especially as these companies go public.**

## Speaker notes

The pattern to teach: a silent price increase. OpenAI kept the sticker price and cut the allowance — Pro 200 subscribers still pay $200 but get half the usage, and the generous allowance now lives on the $500 tier. Pro 500 is also the only Pro tier with Ultrafast.

This follows directly from the ZDR-or-local-AI discussion: hosted tiers are getting more expensive per unit of work at the same time local AI carries its own real bill — hardware, operations, and capacity. There is no free option; the choice is which bill you pay and for what.

The timeline shows the sequence: scarcity (pause sign-ups), then a more expensive option appears, then existing subscribers absorb the reduction. That is what pricing pressure looks like from the inside.

Why it will continue: these companies face enormous compute bills and, as they IPO, pressure to show revenue and margin. Cutting allowances or raising prices is the easiest lever. Assume AI costs per seat trend up, not down.

Practical advice for the organization: budget for rising costs, track actual usage against allowances, right-size the model or plan to the task — including open-weight and local models from the earlier mitigation — and measure cost per verified result; the expensive tier is wasted on work a cheaper one verifies just as well.

Verify current prices and allowances before presenting; they change frequently.

Transition: close the section by tracing data through a whole workflow.
