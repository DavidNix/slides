# Using company-managed AI

- Work in the right account and workspace.
- Know which information is approved for that service.
- Understand who can access chats, files, and shared projects.
- Check what connected applications can read or change.

**A paid personal account is different from a company-managed workspace.**

## Speaker notes

Use Claude and ChatGPT as the products demonstrated in this section. Staff-facing applications provide a ready-made experience; APIs let developers build model capabilities into company software. Their subscriptions, controls, and terms differ.

Company-managed does not mean every plan has the same controls or every use is approved. Confirm the workspace, administrative ownership, available features, and agreed data handling.

The audience can connect these principles to familiar work in email, documents, spreadsheets, and shared files.

For a Microsoft-specific implementation question, use a direct answer: “I haven't implemented that in the Microsoft stack. I can explain the capability and security considerations; your technical team can confirm the configuration in your environment.”
