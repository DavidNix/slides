# Verification belongs in the workflow

**Generate → verify → approve when needed → act → confirm the outcome**

- Match the check to the consequence of error.
- Define what causes rejection or escalation.
- Measure review and correction time.
- Recheck performance when the system changes.

**Time saved = manual effort avoided − review and rework.**

*Remember the freight claim? Let's check its work.*

## Speaker notes

Name the reviewer and source of truth before you deploy.

Test normal cases, missing information, and conflicting sources first. Track accepted results and errors, not response counts. A model or prompt change means re-test.

After a tool acts, check the result. "Sent" from the assistant is not proof of delivery.

Removing review saves visible effort and moves trust to the system. Make that choice on purpose.
