# Local AI: the only guarantee of ZDR

- **Models are just files, not programs.** Run them inside your boundary and nothing leaves.
- The strongest boundary: prompts and documents never need to leave your control.
- Be precise about what "local" covers. A local assistant can still call hosted search, document extraction, or telemetry.
- And there is a real bill: hardware, memory, capacity, updates, and support.

**Only local AI guarantees nothing leaves; everything else is a promise in a document.**

## Speaker notes

ZDR is a contract you trust. Local AI is a guarantee. No outside party can keep a copy.

The whole workflow matters. A local assistant can still call hosted search, extraction, or telemetry. Files, backups, and internal access need their own controls.

Bigger models need more memory and compute, plus capacity planning, updates, and support. A small local model that does one task well is often enough.

Transition: the other side is cost. Hosted allowances are shrinking at the same price.
