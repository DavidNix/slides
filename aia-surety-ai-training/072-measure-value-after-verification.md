# Measure value after verification

- Time per completed task, including review and rework.
- Accuracy, omissions, and useful accepted outputs.
- Cost per verified result.
- Exceptions, unauthorized actions, and operational effort.

**A fast draft is valuable when it leads to better completed work.**

## Speaker notes

Establish the current process as a baseline, then compare a small representative pilot. Include ordinary cases, incomplete inputs, and situations where the correct response is to ask for help.

Illustration, not an AIA estimate: a 12-minute manual task becomes four minutes of assisted work, three minutes of review, and one minute of average rework. The net saving is four minutes. Across 20 tasks, that is 80 minutes—not the eight minutes per task implied by ignoring verification.

Track whether accuracy and service improve as well as speed. A workflow that saves time by skipping necessary checks has changed the risk, not simply improved productivity.

Choose a named owner and a review point before expanding access, users, or unattended operation.
