# Familiar fraud, more convincing packaging

AI can help attackers imitate writing, voices, and documents.

- Verify consequential requests through a known channel.
- Check unexpected recipient or payment changes.
- Pause a suspicious workflow and contact the responsible team.

**A convincing message is not proof of authority.**

## Speaker notes

Keep the example ordinary: someone appears to be a familiar colleague and asks for an urgent change to a destination or access permission. AI can make the packaging more convincing; independent verification is still useful.

Use established contact information rather than a number or link supplied in the suspicious request. Follow AIA's actual reporting process; confirm the contact before presenting rather than inventing one.

For an AI workflow that misbehaves, pause further actions, contact the system owner, and preserve relevant logs through the organization's process. The immediate goal is to stop compounding the event and establish what happened.

Close the section: convenience is valuable, but a skipped check is still a skipped check.
