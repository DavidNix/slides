# AI at Work: Agents & Information Security

**What agents can do, what can go wrong, and how to put them to work safely.**

David Nix · Applied AI for businesses

3 hours · Demonstrations · Discussion
## Speaker notes

Welcome the room. Explain that the goal is to understand AI well enough to use it thoughtfully, recognize useful work, and ask better questions about risk.

Invite questions at any point: "Please ask as we go — you don't need to wait for a pause." There are no separate Q&A slides; take questions at natural boundaries instead.

Introduce the recurring question: **What convenience are we gaining, and what access or control are we giving up?** This is familiar security thinking applied to a new kind of software.
