# The final check is you

- **Verification ends with a person.** Deterministic checks and a second model narrow the problems; neither accepts the work. Judgment does.
- **It depends on the risk.** Start with one question: what could go wrong if this output is wrong?
- **A large consequence needs a human decision.** Money moves, external messages, claim decisions, anything hard to undo. A person decides.
- **Most things need a human decision.** Approval, correction, or rejection by a person is the one control that fits every task.

**The model can produce; only you can accept. Verification is your judgment, sized to the risk.**

## Speaker notes

No tool in this section removes the person. Each one shrinks what the reviewer must look for.

Size the review to the risk. A draft only you read needs a skim. A payment, external message, or claim decision needs a person who reads the evidence and decides.

Accountability lives with a person. Name who accepts each output before the workflow runs.
