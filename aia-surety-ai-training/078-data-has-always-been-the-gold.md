# Data has always been the gold

Models are becoming commodities. Data is the moat:

- Training data, usage data, and your prompts all carry value.
- Providers learn from what users type — every prompt is a signal.
- Your business data is the asset everyone else's model wants.

**If data is the gold, decide who gets to pan your stream.**

## Speaker notes

Frame the economics: compute and algorithms converge across providers; what differentiates products is data. Training data shaped the model; usage data improves the next version. This is why chat products are cheap or free — conversations are worth something to the vendor.

That value flows both ways. The material your staff types into a chatbot — claim details, procedures, customer language — is exactly what a provider wants more of. Treat every prompt as information that leaves your boundary unless policy says otherwise.
