# Always-on agents: your personal chief of staff

Personal assistants that work while you sleep:

- **Dots (OpenAI)** — each dot gets its own cloud computer and browser and connects to thousands of apps.
- **Grok Bot (xAI)** and **Claude Duet (Anthropic)** — proactive agents that keep a goal, memory, and app connections between conversations.
- They start work without being asked: briefings, drafts, reminders, follow-ups.

**The lineage: the self-hosted OpenClaw and Hermes experiments proved people want an always-on agent.**

## Speaker notes

Before the big labs shipped these, two self-hosted projects showed the demand. OpenClaw is a personal assistant that lives in your chat apps and keeps the agent fenced inside its own managed workspace. Hermes is an agent built to remember you and improve the longer you use it. Hobbyists ran them 24/7 and learned the hard lessons: access alone does not make an assistant useful — memory, boundaries, and judgment do.

In 2026 the major labs productized the pattern: OpenAI's Dots, xAI's Grok Bot, Anthropic's Claude Duet, alongside Gemini Spark and Meta's Muse. These are proactive agents: they hold goals between conversations, watch for moments to act, and reach you where you already chat.

Connect the security section at full strength: an always-on agent with your email, calendar, and files is maximum convenience and maximum blast radius. The same questions apply — what authority did you delegate, what does it touch, and how do you audit it?

Choose by the job, not the brand.
