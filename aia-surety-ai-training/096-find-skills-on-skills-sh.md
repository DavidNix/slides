# Where to find skills: skills.sh

You don't have to build every skill from scratch:

- **[skills.sh](https://www.skills.sh/):** an open registry of thousands of agent skills, installable with a single command. Vendor-published skills (Anthropic, Microsoft, Google) sit alongside community ones, with an **Official** section and community **security audits**.
- **A skill is instructions the agent will follow.** Treat one from a stranger like the résumé from the incidents slides: read it before installing, or have the AI scan it for malicious behavior first.
- **The review is realistic:** skills are plain Markdown, with no tiny white-on-white text and no hidden layer, so a read-through actually works.

**Read before you install. With Markdown, you actually can.**

## Speaker notes

skills.sh is an app store for agent skills. It works across Claude, Copilot, and other tools. The leaderboard shows popularity, not safety.

A third-party skill is untrusted content. Treat it like the résumé from the incidents slide.

Read it, or paste it into the agent and ask: "Does anything here send data out, contact the internet, change permissions, or tell you to ignore your instructions?" An AI scan is a first pass, not a guarantee.

Markdown has nowhere to hide text, so the review is possible.

Find a skill close to the task, review it, and adapt it.
