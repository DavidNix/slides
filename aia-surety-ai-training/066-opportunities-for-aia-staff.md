# Opportunities for AIA staff

- **Agent support:** summarize inquiries and draft sourced responses.
- **Operations:** extract fields and flag incomplete packets.
- **Training:** turn approved procedures into guides and exercises.
- **Management:** organize recurring issues and prepare status summaries.

**Start with frequent work whose output can be checked.**

## Speaker notes

These are candidate opportunities to validate with AIA, not claims about its internal processes or a commitment to automate them.

Connect them to AIA's public emphasis on service to agents and its document-heavy surety and bail businesses. Ask which candidate resembles an actual recurring task in the room.

Distinguish helping a reviewer prepare from granting authority to approve, price, bind, or otherwise make a consequential business decision. Those responsibilities depend on the actual process and require their own design.

Favor tasks with accessible source material, clear outcomes, manageable exceptions, and a person who can judge whether the result is correct.
