# Exercise: design one useful pilot

Choose one task. Specify:

1. The convenience or business benefit.
2. The source data and minimum permissions.
3. The evidence that verifies the result.
4. The action that still needs a boundary or review.
5. The owner and measure of success.

**What would make you stop or change the pilot?**

## Speaker notes

Run this in pairs or small groups. It works without laptops or AI accounts.

- **Two minutes:** choose a specific, recurring task from the opening discussion or opportunity slides.
- **Three minutes:** fill in the pilot card below.
- **Two minutes:** hear two short proposals and ask one verification or permissions question about each.

## Pilot card

| Decision | Group's answer |
| --- | --- |
| Task and current pain | What happens today, and how often? |
| Benefit | Which time, quality, or service outcome should improve? |
| Data and destination | Which sources are needed, and where may they be processed? |
| Authority | What may AI read, prepare, send, or change? |
| Verification | Which source, calculation, rule, or reviewer checks the result? |
| Accepted tradeoff | What extra exposure buys the convenience, and what limits it? |
| Owner and success measure | Who operates it, and how will useful results be measured? |
| Stop condition | Which errors or incidents cause a pause? |

Encourage a narrow first version. The order-status example needs two read-only lookups and a short answer, not unrestricted control over an entire account.
