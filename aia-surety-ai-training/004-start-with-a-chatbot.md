# Start with a chatbot

You ask. It responds. You refine the request.

- **AI:** a broad family of systems for tasks such as prediction, recognition, and generation.
- **Generative AI:** produces content, including text, images, and audio.
- **Language model:** generates language from patterns learned during training and the context supplied now.

## Speaker notes

Open a chat app. Show the experience before the machinery.

Separate three names: OpenAI is a company, ChatGPT is an app, and the app uses models. Claude names both Anthropic's app and its models.

A chat box tells you nothing about what access the system has.

Ask: "When you type a question here, what can the software see?"
